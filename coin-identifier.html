Mint mark key

CC = Carson City  •  O = New Orleans  •  S = San Francisco  •  D = Denver  •  W = West Point  •  P / none = Philadelphia

Tips for accurate reading

Use a 5–10× loupe under angled light. On Morgans, the mint mark is tiny (~1 mm) — beware added or altered mint marks on key dates like 1889-CC and 1893-S. On an authentic 1893-S, the date's "1" aligns with a denticle and the S is level; added mint marks often show a seam or wrong position.
